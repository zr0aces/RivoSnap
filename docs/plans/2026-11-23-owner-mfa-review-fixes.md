In scope: `internal/api/security.go`, `internal/api/totp.go`, `internal/api/server.go` (`issueSession` split only), their tests, `apps/web/src/main.tsx`, `apps/web/src/profile.tsx`, `apps/web/src/style.css`, `e2e/*`, and docs. Out of scope: new authentication features, Redis-backed rate limiting, changing the TOTP or WebAuthn libraries.
